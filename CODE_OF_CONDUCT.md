# Contributor Covenant 3.0 Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for all.

We are committed to fostering an environment that respects and promotes the
dignity, rights, and contributions of all individuals, regardless of
characteristics including race, ethnicity, caste, color, age, physical
characteristics, neurodiversity, disability, sex or gender, gender identity or
expression, sexual orientation, language, philosophy or religion, national or
social origin, socio-economic position, level of education, or other status.
The same privileges of participation are extended to everyone who participates
in good faith and in accordance with this Covenant.

## Encouraged behaviors

While acknowledging differences in social norms, we all strive to meet our
community's expectations for positive behavior. We also understand that our
words and actions may be interpreted differently than we intend based on
culture, background, or native language.

With these considerations in mind, we agree to behave mindfully toward each
other and act in ways that center our shared values, including:

1. Respecting the purpose of our community, our activities, and our ways of
   gathering.
2. Engaging kindly and honestly with others.
3. Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.
4. Taking responsibility for our actions and contributions.
5. Gracefully giving and accepting constructive feedback.
6. Committing to repairing harm when it occurs.
7. Behaving in other ways that promote and sustain the well-being of our
   community.

## Restricted behaviors

We agree to restrict the following behaviors in our community. Instances,
threats, and promotion of these behaviors are violations of this Code of
Conduct.

1. **Harassment.** Violating explicitly expressed boundaries or engaging in
   unnecessary personal attention after any clear request to stop.
2. **Character attacks.** Making insulting, demeaning, or pejorative comments
   directed at a community member or group of people.
3. **Stereotyping or discrimination.** Characterizing anyone's personality or
   behavior on the basis of immutable identities or traits.
4. **Sexualization.** Behaving in a way that would generally be considered
   inappropriately intimate in the context or purpose of the community.
5. **Violating confidentiality.** Sharing or acting on someone's personal or
   private information without their permission.
6. **Endangerment.** Causing, encouraging, or threatening violence or other
   harm toward any person or group.
7. Behaving in other ways that **threaten the well-being** of our community.

### Other restrictions

1. **Misleading identity.** Impersonating someone else for any reason, or
   pretending to be someone else to evade enforcement actions.
2. **Failing to credit sources.** Not properly crediting the sources of content
   you contribute.
3. **Promotional materials.** Sharing marketing or other commercial content in
   a way that is outside the norms of the community.
4. **Irresponsible communication.** Failing to responsibly present content
   which includes, links, or describes any other restricted behaviors.

## Reporting an issue

Not every disagreement is a Code of Conduct violation. The encouraged
behaviors above can help participants resolve ordinary conflicts. If you
experience or witness conduct that may violate this policy, email
[dev@dopbase.com](mailto:dev@dopbase.com). Do not report conduct violations in
a public issue, discussion, or pull request.

Include the relevant dates, community spaces, people involved, and any links
or other records that may help the review. Share only information you have
permission to disclose. Security vulnerabilities should follow the private
process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead.

Dopbase maintainers will review reports and investigate as needed. They may
review messages, logs, or other records and speak with participants or
witnesses. Report details will be shared only with people who need them to
investigate, protect participants, or apply an enforcement action.

A maintainer involved in a report must not take part in its review or
resolution. If no unconflicted maintainer is available, an independent
external reviewer will handle the report. Enforcement actions will usually be
handled privately. A public statement may be part of a resolution when doing
so does not compromise safety or confidentiality.

## Addressing and repairing harm

If an investigation finds a violation, Dopbase maintainers may use the
following enforcement ladder. They will consider the incident's impact on the
people involved and the community. Serious violations may skip earlier steps.

1. Warning
   1. Event: a violation involving one incident or a series of incidents.
   2. Consequence: a private written warning from the maintainers.
   3. Repair: possible steps include a private written apology,
      acknowledgement of responsibility, and clarification of expectations.
2. Temporarily limited activities
   1. Event: a repeated violation that previously resulted in a warning, or a
      first occurrence of a more serious violation.
   2. Consequence: a private written warning and a time-limited cooldown. The
      restriction may apply to particular channels or interactions.
   3. Repair: possible steps include an apology, reflection during the
      cooldown, and careful re-entry into community spaces afterward.
3. Temporary suspension
   1. Event: repeated violations that warnings have not resolved, or one
      serious violation.
   2. Consequence: a private written notice that states the suspension and the
      conditions for return.
   3. Repair: respect the suspension, meet the stated conditions, and return
      to the community thoughtfully when the suspension ends.
4. Permanent ban
   1. Event: repeated violations that other steps have not resolved, or a
      violation so serious that the community cannot remain safe while the
      person continues to participate.
   2. Consequence: removal of access to community spaces, tools, and
      communication channels.
   3. Repair: no return to the community is available for a violation at this
      level.

This ladder is a guideline. Maintainers may use their judgment to choose an
action that protects the community and addresses the harm caused.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all Dopbase community spaces. These include
repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-managed
communication channels. It also applies when someone officially represents
Dopbase, such as by using an official email address or social media account or
acting as an appointed representative at an online or in-person event.
